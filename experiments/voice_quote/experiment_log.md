# Experiment Log — Voice→Quote

## Rules

- Record **only what actually happened**, timestamped `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM SAST`.
- Every entry must reference evidence: a file in `evidence/` or an exact quoted message.
  No evidence → no entry.
- **No interpretation, no prediction, no "this suggests…"** — that belongs in the
  Decision section at the end, and only on measured numbers.
- A revenue entry requires a payment-receipt path in `evidence/`.
  No receipt → the tracker's revenue field stays blank and no revenue is logged here.

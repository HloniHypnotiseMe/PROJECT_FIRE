# Outreach — Voice→Quote Concierge (WhatsApp / DM)

## Rules (non-negotiable)

- **Maximum 2 follow-ups** after the first message. No third. No response → stop, log it.
- Personalise: name + trade + suburb if known.
- **≤3 lines per message.** No jargon, no "AI", no "startup", no "revolution".
- The only CTA in the whole experiment: *send a voice note about a real job you'd need to quote.*
- Never invent urgency ("last 2 spots") and never promise jobs, clients, or income.
- A "no" or "stop" = log it and send nothing more, ever.
- Log every send and reply with timestamps in `prospect_tracker.csv` + `experiment_log.md`.
- Language: English first. If they reply in isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sepedi, Sesotho or
  Tswana, continue in their language — offer unchanged.

## S1 — First touch

> Hi [NAME], it's [YOUR NAME] — I work with [TRADE]s around [SUBURB].
> I'm testing something: you send me a voice note about a job, and I send back a clean,
> itemised PDF quote within 5 minutes — ready to forward to your client.
> Free for 14 days while I test it. Want to try one on a real job this week?

## S2 — Follow-up 1 (24–48h if no reply)

> Hi [NAME], no pressure. If a job lands this week, just voice-note me the details and I'll
> produce the quote for you, free. If it's not useful, I won't bother you again.

## S3 — Follow-up 2 (day 4–5, last contact)

> [NAME], I'll close your file. If you ever want a quote produced from a voice note in
> 5 minutes, you know where to find me. Good luck this week.

## S4 — When they agree to a trial

> Good. Here's how it works: when a job comes in, voice-note me (1) what the work is,
> (2) where, (3) the materials if you know them, (4) how urgent. I'll send the quote
> within 5 minutes. First one's free — just send your first job when it lands.

## S5 — Common questions

- **"How much will it cost?"**
  > Free for the 14 days while I test. After that it's R199/month — and if it isn't worth
  > that to you, I'll tell you before you pay.
  (Then, only if/when the trial ends, follow the P1 → P2 → P3 ladder in the README.
  Never open with P2 or P3.)

- **"Will it sound like a robot wrote it?"**
  > No — I write it. You send the voice note, it comes out with your business name and
  > itemised prices. You can edit anything before you send it on.

- **"I'm too busy."**
  > That's exactly the point. It takes 30 seconds to send a voice note — and if I can't
  > save you time on one quote, the trial's a waste of both our time.

## Anti-patterns (do not do)

- No bulk or group messages. One person, one thread.
- No "Dear valued business" templates.
- No claims like "this will get you more jobs" — the only claim ever made:
  **a professional quote within 5 minutes of your voice note.**
- No sharing prospect details with anyone.
- No quoting a price in S1. Price is discussed only at trial end.

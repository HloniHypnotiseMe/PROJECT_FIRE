### Late payments (grounds #10)
- 91% of SA SMEs affected by late payments; invoices paid on average 18 days late (2025 Xero SA Late Payments Report) [1](https://smesouthafrica.co.za/how-smes-can-improve-cash-flow-through-accounting/)

## 4. Target Customer (per top-3)

1. **Loom → SOP:** ops leads & founders in 5–50 person startups, globally, who onboard staff with Loom videos.
2. **WA Voice-Quote:** SA plumbers, electricians, and handymen running one-person businesses off WhatsApp Business, losing jobs to slower formal quoting.
3. **Tenant Demand Letter:** residential landlords (individual owners, JHB/CPT) managing non-paying tenants without lawyers.

---

## 5. Problem

1. **Loom → SOP:** every walkthrough is re-recorded because it was never converted into a documented, versioned SOP; tribal knowledge dies with the employee.
2. **WA Voice-Quote:** a tradesperson's voice note is where the job is won, but the formal quote they need to close it takes them an evening to type; customers ghost on verbal-only estimates.
3. **Tenant Demand Letter:** landlords must issue a legally-valid demand letter before escalation, but lawyers charge R1,500+ and DIY templates cite the wrong Act, which landlords discover only after the eviction stalls.

---

## 6. Proposed Solution

1. **Loom → SOP:** paste a Loom link → transcription → AI-segmented, step-by-step Notion SOP with screenshots placeholders, reviewed by a human pass in v1.
2. **WA Voice-Quote:** tradesperson forwards a voice note → transcription → itemised branded PDF quote returned in minutes; concierge-human in week 1, API-automated afterwards.
3. **Tenant Demand Letter:** paste WhatsApp chat + lease details → POPIA-safe template engine produces a demand letter citing the correct section (Rental Housing Act / CPA), lawyer-reviewed before release.

---

## 7. Validation Experiment (first 14 days)

Run in parallel only for the top 2. Each experiment must produce a binary outcome.

**Experiment A — WA Voice-Quote (days 1–7):** manually run the concierge flow for 10 JHB plumbers found in trade WhatsApp groups. Success = ≥5 send a second quote request AND ≥2 pay R199 after a 14-day trial. Kill = <5 first-touch requests or concierge turnaround >30 min. This validates pain, willingness to pay, and channel in one week — before any code is written.

**Experiment B — Loom → SOP (days 8–14):** post the offer to 3 founder communities (LinkedIn + Product Hunt style); give 10 teams the service free in exchange for a renewal-commitment pre-purchase at $15/mo. Success = ≥5 pre-purchase and ≥3 teams use it weekly. Kill = <40% trial→paid conversion or SOP quality below a manual baseline.

**Cross-cutting quality gate (Reality Engine):** every artifact produced in the experiments is scored by `testing/reality-checker` with GO / REVISE / NO-GO before it ships to a customer.
